01 / Product definition

Product overview

The PDI-12 is a passive, transformer-isolated direct box intended for active instruments, keyboards and unbalanced line sources. It has no battery and no phantom-power circuit.

Its design prioritises a premium transformer, restrained wiring and mechanically supported panel parts to improve reliability and repairability.

The release is suitable as an editable design and prototype / RFQ package, but the report explicitly states that no physical unit has yet been built.

Important design limitation

This is not a 1 MΩ active DI. The pad network is intended for active / line sources. Passive pickups can experience audible loading, especially with a low-impedance thru amplifier, and high-impedance piezo sources are unsuitable without a buffer.

Transformer & loading

A 1 kΩ secondary load reflects to about 144 kΩ for an ideal 12:1 transformer. The manufacturer specifies approximately 141 kΩ input impedance and 150 Ω output impedance in its stated test circuit; those values are transformer test data, not measurements of this completed box.

Pad network

R1 = 9090 Ω series and R2 = 1000 Ω shunt. In BYPASS, the shunt lower end is open; in PAD, R2 is grounded. The report calculates about −20.17 dB incremental pad loss with a 50 Ω source.

Source sensitivity

Calculated incremental pad attenuation is about −20.89 dB with a 1 kΩ source and about −25.52 dB with a 10 kΩ source. This is why source/load fixture conditions matter during qualification.

Grounding, isolation & robustness

• XLR pin 2 is hot (T1 RED); pin 3 is cold (BRN).

• XLR pin 1 and shell stay connected to chassis in both switch positions.

• T1 WHI goes to the chassis network. T1 ORG, BLK and GRY go to instrument signal ground.

• SW2 grounds the instrument network in GROUND and disconnects it in LIFT.

• The GRY lead is bonded to the transformer can; the can must not touch the enclosure, metal clips or grounded fasteners.

• Two #4 Type B screws provide 3.025 mm calculated penetration; vendor limit is 3.81 mm maximum.

• Unused switch contacts are covered; heat shrink and nylon clips provide solder-joint strain relief.

• Bonding areas remain bare under washers and conductive lid contact is checked after coating.

Manufacturing & deliverables

The engineering report is structured as a prototype / quotation release. These details are the intended outsourced manufacturing basis.

PCB fabrication

  • 2-layer FR4, 1.6 mm ±0.16 mm
  • 1 oz copper per side
  • Lead-free HASL, green solder mask, white top silk
  • 0.40 mm design traces / 0.30 mm minimum clearance
  • Finished solder holes 1.0 mm; resistor holes 0.8 mm
  • Vias 0.4 mm; mounting holes 3.2 mm

Mechanical fabrication

  • Base: 6061-T6 aluminium, 150 × 100 × 50 mm body
  • 3 mm walls, 5 mm floor; integral lid bosses
  • 6 mm end-mill-suitable R3 pocket corners
  • 3 mm lid and 6 mm acetal carrier machined separately
  • Drawing callouts specify M3 threads and depths
  • Deburr 0.2–0.5 mm and remove chips before electronics assembly

Manufacturing strategy note

Review before batch

The custom CNC case makes RFQ geometry unambiguous and avoids assuming a stock casting is dimensionally interchangeable, but it is expensive for a basic DI. A suitable die-cast case could reduce commercial-volume machining cost, but any substitution requires new mechanical drawings, clearance checks and first-article approval. The transformer is also a major cost driver; any cheaper transformer requires complete audio requalification.

08 / First article

First-article qualification & acceptance

Use calibrated instruments. Record source level, frequency, load, pad position and lift position for every result. These are provisional acceptance conditions until the first article establishes measured, justified production limits.

09 / Compliance intent

Standards & process intentions

Workmanship

Specify IPC-A-610 Class 2 workmanship as the contract acceptance objective and use a lead-free process with supplier material declarations for relevant market requirements.

Grounding practice

The pin 1-at-chassis topology follows established professional audio grounding practice, but the report explicitly treats this as a design intention rather than an AES48 conformity declaration.

Compliance status

EMC, regulatory labelling and environmental compliance still need assessment for the intended market. No certification is claimed by these files.

Safety boundary

No mains isolation, protective-earth function or safety certification is claimed. Never lift the protective earth of connected powered equipment.

10 / Release control

Opening the project & controlling the release

Project opening

  1. Extract the ZIP into one folder and open DI_Box.kicad_pro in KiCad 7 or newer.
  2. Project-local symbol and footprint libraries use ${KIPRJMOD}; no downloaded commercial CAD libraries are required.
  3. If a newer KiCad version offers migration, work on a copy when preserving the release.
  4. The design uses no SMT parts; THT_insertion.csv is a manual insertion reference, not a pick-and-place file.

Before manufacturing

  1. Run schematic ERC and PCB DRC in the installed KiCad version.
  2. Review the schematic-to-PCB changes dialog.
  3. Inspect Gerbers and both drill files in a Gerber viewer.
  4. Obtain supplier drawings for purchased parts.
  5. Check delivered switch terminal numbering / orientation.
  6. Complete first-article qualification before ordering a batch.
  7. Keep BOM, harness and drawings at the same revision after any change.
Release gate
Do not release a production batch until the first article passes the approved acceptance plan.
